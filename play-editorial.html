Stephy Pak ← Back to work

Hi, I'm Stephy. I design brands, but mostly I'm curious about how people live, dress and spend their time.

I feel electrified wandering new cities, islands and the side streets nobody put on the map, and I always come home with a camera roll full of shop signs, packaging and street style.

Fashion is how I read a place. I work in luxury retail, and I love the details people feel before they notice them: how a store sounds, how a bag gets wrapped, what a colour says before anyone speaks. (For the record, I'm a True Summer.)

Wherever I am, I go looking for galleries, record shops, zines and pop-ups, and I keep the small stuff: tickets, tags, receipts with good type. Some of it is in the photos below.

I don't think there's anything wrong with eating well, dressing well and exploring the world. It's never a waste. What I love about design is getting into the things that shape those experiences.

And outside of all that, I'm following the music ♫
